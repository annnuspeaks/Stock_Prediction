# Target users
Individual investors, students/researchers, and general users who want data-driven stock analysis and decision support without needing to interpret complex market data themselves.

# Scope and limitations
Nexora will focus on stock analysis, market-data processing, prediction, risk assessment, and BUY/SELL/WATCH/HOLD/NO SIGNAL decision support. It will not guarantee returns, provide personalized financial advice, or execute trades automatically in the initial version.

# Initial supported market/region
Indian equity market — NSE/BSE listed stocks.

This gives us a clear initial boundary. We can expand to other markets later if needed.

# Prediction horizons
1 trading day and 1 trading week for the initial version.
